Private Tours · Central West End

Eat Like a Lou-natic, on your schedule

Our public tour runs Thursday through Saturday at 2pm. Can’t make that work? We’ll run our acclaimed food tour just for your group — any day, any time that works for you.

The same tour, just your people

Breakfast, lunch, dinner and dessert in one afternoon — a full spread of St. Louis classics, every one of them a hometown invention, served at air-conditioned sit-down stops along a flat, easy walk through the Central West End. In between, a local guide tells the good, bad and complicated history of the neighborhood St. Louis built at its peak. Book it privately and the whole table is yours.

Your day, your time

Mornings, weekdays, Sundays — tell us when you want to go and we’ll work with our restaurant partners to make it happen.

Only your group

No strangers at the table. Inside jokes welcome. The pace and the conversation are built around the people you bring.

Room to celebrate

Birthdays, anniversaries, proposals, retirements — let us know what you’re marking and we’ll help make the afternoon feel like an occasion.

Who books it

  • Corporate outings, team-building and client entertaining
  • Birthday, bachelorette and bachelor parties
  • Family reunions and visiting relatives
  • Convention, wedding and conference groups looking for a real taste of St. Louis
  • Clubs, alumni groups and anyone who’d rather not share the guide

Rated 4.8 out of 5 by guests and featured in St. Louis Magazine.
